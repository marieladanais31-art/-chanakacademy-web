INSTITUTIONAL VALUE

Why Partner with Chanak?

Establishing an independent accredited school requires years of bureaucratic approvals, curriculum design, and technological overhead. A Chanak partnership allows you to launch a high-caliber accredited microschool from day one.

🏛️

Chanak International Academy is registered with the Florida Department of Education (FLDOE #134620) and fully accredited by the Middle States Association (MSA-CESS). Enrolled students earn official transcripts and a recognized American High School Diploma.

💳

In the United States, we manage direct invoicing and authorizations through state scholarship platforms (Step Up For Students, ClassWallet, Odyssey), providing seamless financial access for eligible families.

SAFETY & RIGOR STANDARDS

Operational & Facility Requirements

To safeguard student safety and preserve educational excellence, all partner organizations must satisfy the following criteria:

1. Background Checks

Verified criminal background screenings (and child-protection clearances) for all staff, volunteers, and personnel accessing the learning facility.

2. Liability Insurance

Active General Liability Insurance policy covering the physical premises and educational activities conducted on-site.

3. Coordinator Certification

Mandatory successful completion of Chanak's pedagogical certification program by the designated cohort Coordinator prior to student enrollment.

4. Local Code Compliance

Full compliance with municipal zoning, fire safety, and building codes applicable to the facility in the local jurisdiction.

5. International Requirement (Outside the U.S.)

For locations in Spain, Mexico, Panama, and other countries, the partner organization must possess official authorization and applicable legal compliance in its home country for operating in-person services with youth.

PARTNERSHIP PROCESS

Partner Roadmap: From Concept to Opening

A disciplined six-phase roadmap designed to ensure seamless launch and academic viability.

Step 01

Self-Assessment Test

Complete our 5-question compatibility test below to evaluate preliminary fit for your organization and community.

Step 02

Discovery Call

A 30-minute consultation with leadership to discuss your community goals, address questions, and provide the institutional dossier.

Step 03

Site & Entity Review

Partnership Agreement

Execution of the formal Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) defining roles, deliverables, and operational parameters.

Step 05

Coordinator Certification

Comprehensive training of designated Coordinators on Mastery Learning, SIS administration, and cohort dynamics.

Step 06

Cohort Launch

Official opening supported by Chanak's academic office, ongoing IEP coordination, and transcript stewardship throughout the academic year.

PRELIMINARY ASSESSMENT

Partner Compatibility Test

Check in less than two minutes whether your organization satisfies the core pillars to launch a Chanak Microschool.

1. Do you possess an independent legal entity (private school, church, foundation, association, or corporate body) or registered institutional structure?
2. Do you have an adequate physical space (or one under preparation) with reliable high-speed internet, safety, and capacity for 5 to 12 students?
3. Have you identified an educator or vocational mentor prepared to complete Chanak's official Coordinator training and certification?
4. Do you have an established community of interested families (for example, 10 or more) in K–5, 6–8, or 9–12?
5. Are you committed to maintaining child-protection standards (background checks, liability insurance, and local municipal compliance)?
