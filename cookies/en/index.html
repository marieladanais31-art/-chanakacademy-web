Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small files or similar technologies stored on or read from the user's device to enable technical functions, remember preferences or measure how a website is used.

Technical or necessary cookies

They are required for the basic operation of the site, navigation, security and keeping essential preferences. These cookies do not require consent because they are indispensable in order to provide the requested service.

Analytics and measurement cookies

They may be used to understand how the site is visited, measure campaigns and improve content or user experience. They are only enabled if the user accepts cookies.

Advertising cookies and Google Ads

Google Tag / Google Ads cookies or signals may be used to measure advertising conversions or improve campaign relevance. These functions must only be enabled in accordance with the consent given by the user.

How to manage consent

On your first visit you can accept, reject or configure cookies from the banner. If you accept, Google Tag will update consent to analytics_storage="granted" y ad_storage="granted". If you reject, it stays at analytics_storage="denied" y ad_storage="denied".
