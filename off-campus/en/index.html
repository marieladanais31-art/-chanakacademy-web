FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What we are asked most

?Can I start at any time of the year?

Yes. The Mastery Learning model allows you to join at any time. There is no fixed date and no waiting period.

?Is the American diploma internationally valid?

Chanak issues diplomas from a school registered in Florida (FLDOE #134620). The documentation is apostillable.

?Do I need to live in a specific country?

No. We operate 100% online. Families in Spain, Panama, Colombia, Mexico and many other countries take part.

?Where can I find the prices?

You can review them in the pricing section of this page. The information dossier expands on the admission process and the academic path.

?What does enrollment include?

Enrollment includes the academic diagnostic and the IEP, so the student's record is opened correctly.

?What is Mastery Learning?

The student advances once they master the content — not by putting in time. It guarantees there are no learning gaps.
