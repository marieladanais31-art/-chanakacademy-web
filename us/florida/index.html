Florida Families / PEP & ESA Notice

Educational Reimbursement & Itemized Invoices

For Florida families participating in Education Savings Accounts (ESA / PEP / Step Up for Students):

  • Chanak provides official, itemized invoices detailing distance learning curriculum, instructional platform access, and academic diagnostic assessments.
  • Parents can submit documentation directly to their scholarship management portal for eligible educational expense reimbursement according to Florida state program guidelines.

*Note: Chanak operates as a distance learning private school umbrella. Reimbursement eligibility is determined by the scholarship funding organization (SFO).
