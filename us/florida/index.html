What is an umbrella school — and why Florida families need one

An umbrella school (also called a cover school, or escuela sombrilla in Spanish) is a registered private school that enrolls your homeschooled child officially, keeps the academic record and issues the transcript. You teach at home; the school stands behind the paperwork.

Chanak International Academy is registered with the Florida Department of Education as a private school (FLDOE #134620) and is an official candidate for accreditation with the Middle States Association Commissions on Elementary and Secondary Schools (MSA-CESS). That means your child is enrolled in a real school with an official file — not in a curriculum subscription.

For Hispanic families it solves two problems at once: your child keeps Spanish, faith and family culture at home, and still graduates with a U.S. High School diploma and transcript that colleges recognize.

State scholarships and education savings accounts

Several states now fund part or all of a family's education expenses. What each program covers differs, and the rules change every legislative session, so here is where Chanak actually stands.

Part-time programs

Our Dual Diploma, individual courses and Academic English are part-time and are the kind of expense several state programs cover as curriculum or part-time instruction. Ask your scholarship funding organization whether Chanak can be reimbursed under your account.

Full-time distance instruction

Florida's choice scholarships do not treat full-time online instruction from a private school as an eligible expense. Our full-time K-12 program is paid directly by the family, or supported through Chanak Foundation aid.

What we can do today

We issue itemized invoices with our FLDOE registration number, enrollment records and transcripts, which is what most scholarship organizations require for reimbursement. Eligibility is always determined by the funding organization, never by us.

Frequently asked questions

Is Chanak accredited?

Chanak International Academy is registered as a private school with the Florida Department of Education (FLDOE #134620, publicly verifiable) and is an official candidate for accreditation with the Middle States Association Commissions on Elementary and Secondary Schools. The validation visit has been completed with a favorable recommendation from the visiting committee; the commission's decision is expected in November 2026. Chanak is not accredited by MSA yet.

Who issues the diploma and the transcript?

Chanak does. We enroll the student, hold the academic record and issue the official U.S. transcript and the American High School Diploma. Each college and university sets its own admission and credit-recognition requirements.

Does my child need to speak English already?

No. The Academic Diagnostic places the student at their real level, and the Academic English Pathway builds from there. Family support is bilingual throughout.

Can we enroll mid-year?

Yes. Enrollment is open year-round and the individualized learning plan is built from where the student actually is, not from a fixed calendar.

Is this a homeschool program or a school?

It is a school. Families school at home, but the student is enrolled in a registered private school that keeps the record and issues the credentials.

Is financial aid available?

The Chanak Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, awards need-based scholarships through an application and a selection committee, with a defined annual budget. Ask Admissions for the current criteria and deadlines.
