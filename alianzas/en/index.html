FOUR WAYS TO COLLABORATE

An agreement for each institutional goal

Academic collaboration keeps the local institution’s identity. Implementing an affiliated in-person centre requires a different framework and operational review.

01

Academic certification and collaboration

For centres with their own educational identity.

Learning assessment and audit, evidence review, academic records and documentation for approved centres and students.

Academic Collaboration Agreement
What we define together

Review of curriculum, evidence and responsibilities. Documents and credits depend on evaluation and the agreed scope.

02

Institutional Dual Diploma

For schools adding a U.S. academic pathway.

Students retain their local school and curriculum while adding Chanak’s pathway, with academic follow-up and individual transcript review.

Academic Collaboration Agreement
What we define together

Credit planning, Chanak subjects, school coordination and student guidance.

03

Home education and platform support

For organisations supporting homeschool families.

Chanak’s support environment and academic follow-up, with goals and responsibilities defined for the contracted programme.

Academic Collaboration Agreement
What we define together

Specify who enrols, supervises and maintains records. Applicable where the home education pathway is compatible with local requirements.

04

Partner Learning Center

For an organisation implementing an in-person centre.

An affiliated centre with local implementation under a Framework Agreement and Chanak Central supervision.

Implementation Framework Agreement
What we define together

Organisation and premises review, local permits, team training and operational conditions before opening.

Pathways 1–3 use an Academic Collaboration Agreement; pathway 4 uses an Implementation Framework Agreement. The applicable programme, academic documents and fees are confirmed in the institutional proposal.

SPAIN

Collaboration with EducaFe

In Spain, Chanak maintains an institutional alliance with Asociación Cristiana EducaFe (CIF G 19357789) for educational collaboration with families, communities and projects. The specific collaboration is defined through the relevant agreement.
