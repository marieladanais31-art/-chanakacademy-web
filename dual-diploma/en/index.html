What is the Dual Diploma?

The Dual Diploma lets the student follow a complementary path toward the U.S. diploma while continuing their local education or main academic route.

Who is it for?

ESO and Bachillerato students

Families seeking an international profile

Students who want to strengthen academic English

Families who want a path with real follow-up

Schools interested in broadening their offer

LMS + SIS

The LMS organizes resources, activities and evidence.

The SIS enables academic follow-up, progress, reports, documentation and communication with families.
