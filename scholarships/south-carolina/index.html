About Us

At Chanak TrainUp Education Inc. (d/b/a Chanak International Academy), we walk alongside home-educating families with premier personalized mentoring and holistic character formation.

We anchor character education in a biblical worldview that nurtures integrity, lifelong purpose, and a heart for service in every student.

We implement the 60/20/20 learning model: 60% active conceptual study, 20% personalized academic review and feedback, and 20% practical life skills and leadership development.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to essential questions from families utilizing state education funds.

Can I pay for this service using my South Carolina ESTF funds? +

Chanak TrainUp Education Inc. provides educational support and Life Skills services for families with an awarded ESTF account. We issue a monthly invoice paid via Pay Vendor on ClassWallet. Fund payment depends on service eligibility and available account balance.

What is not included in this service? +

It does not include general curriculum textbooks and workbooks (families purchase them directly with an estimated cost of $300 to $700/year per student). It does not include live English language classes, in-person supervision, or childcare.

Does this support package include an official school transcript and high school diploma? +

This support package does not constitute full school enrollment or independently award graduation credits or a High School diploma. Families receive monthly progress reports and academic progress records. Chanak International Academy operates as a private school in Florida (FLDOE #134620); full diploma-granting enrollment is handled through separate private school enrollment.

What are the live class schedules? +

Live Life Skills workshops and mentoring meetings are scheduled during confirmed class times coordinated with the family. Teachers are not available 24/7, though the virtual learning platform is accessible 24/7.

How are curriculum books and study materials purchased? +

Families purchase general curriculum materials directly from publishers after receiving our recommended list during the initial assessment. Chanak Life Skills program materials are provided at no extra cost.

What is the cancellation and refund policy? +

You may cancel with advance notice prior to the upcoming month. Invoicing is monthly per student via Pay Vendor. If a refund of state funds is due for unrendered services, funds are returned directly to the student's state account in ClassWallet; refunds are never disbursed in cash or to private accounts.

In what language are sessions and academic support delivered? +

Support is fully bilingual. Mentoring, ILP assessments, and family communications are offered in Spanish or English according to family preference. Group Life Skills sessions encourage fluent bilingual communication.
