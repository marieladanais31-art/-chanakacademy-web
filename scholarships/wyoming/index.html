Q: What educational expenses does Wyoming ESA cover at Chanak?

Wyoming ESA funds (up to $7,000 per student) can cover 100% of our Annual Nonpublic Registration ($295), full-year monthly tuition across Elementary ($275/mo), Middle School ($320/mo), and High School ($365/mo), the Diagnostic Placement Evaluation ($200), single elective courses (Spanish $120/mo, Life Skills $85/mo), individualized academic mentorship ($150/mo), and required annual standardized testing.

(Español) Cubre el 100% de la matrícula anual, colegiatura mensual K-12, evaluación diagnóstica, cursos electivos, mentoría académica y recursos digitales.

Q: Do families need to pay upfront out-of-pocket?

No. Families participating in Wyoming ESA should NOT make personal debit/credit card payments. Our team coordinates placement and guides you to authorize direct disbursement within your Odyssey portal directly to CHANAK TRAINUP EDUCATION INC.

(Español) No. No debe pagar con tarjetas personales. Nuestro equipo coordina la matrícula y te guía para autorizar el desembolso directo dentro de tu portal de Odyssey a nombre de CHANAK TRAINUP EDUCATION INC.

Q: How is the American High School diploma accredited and recognized?

Chanak is a registered private school with the Florida Department of Education (FLDOE #134620) and an official Candidate for Accreditation with the Middle States Association Commissions on Elementary and Secondary Schools (MSA-CESS). Graduating students earn an official U.S. High School Diploma and transcripts eligible for apostille under the Hague Convention, accepted by universities across the U.S. and worldwide.

(Español) Los graduados obtienen un Diploma oficial de High School estadounidense y expediente académico con opción de apostilla de La Haya, con pleno reconocimiento universitario.
