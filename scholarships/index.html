FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions About State Education Funds

Clear answers to the most common questions from families using public school choice funds at Chanak International Academy.

? 1. How does payment work with state scholarship funds?

Eligible families may request payment for authorized services using available program funds. Approval depends on each state's program, the specific service, and the student. Chanak does not guarantee coverage or funding amounts. In direct-pay states (such as Arizona, Alabama, South Carolina, or New Hampshire on ClassWallet, and Utah and Wyoming on Odyssey), Chanak provides the itemized quote or invoice for the family to submit directly through their platform.

? 2. How does my family locate Chanak in our portal?

Search for our exact approved legal entity: CHANAK TRAINUP EDUCATION INC. In ClassWallet, look under 'Pay Vendor'; in Odyssey, locate our profile in the official state provider directory; in Florida EMA, select Chanak International Academy under Home Education Instructional Program.

? 3. What if my child's scholarship is approved mid-year?

Chanak maintains rolling admissions throughout the academic year. If your state funds are awarded midway through the year, we establish an individualized service plan and payment schedule aligned with your remaining available balance.

? 4. Do unused funds roll over or transfer to another sibling?

Policies vary by state. Generally, funds awarded to one student cannot be transferred to a sibling, but many state programs allow unused balances to roll over into subsequent academic years. Our team provides state-specific rules.

? 5. Does this support package include official cumulative transcripts and diploma?

In states where Virtual Academic Support is offered, packages provide academic progress records, monthly progress reports, and pedagogical tracking without conferring a graduation diploma. In states where Full-Time Online School is offered (such as Arizona), students are formally enrolled under FLDOE #134620 and high school graduates earn official transcripts and an American High School diploma.

? 6. How are state scholarship services distinguished from private academic programs?

State scholarship funds are applied to contract specific authorized educational services (such as Full-Time Online School in Arizona under Tuition and Fees, or virtual guided academic support packages in other states). Programs operate under their respective authorized state guidelines.

? 7. Does Chanak teach with Christian values?

Yes: a biblical Christian worldview is integrated into the 20% Life Skills component and mentorship sessions, using materials from Christian publishers chosen by the family (such as LIFEPAC, Christian Light, or A.C.E.).

? 8. Do you provide support in Spanish?

Yes: all guided academic support, mentoring sessions, parent communications, and advisement are fully offered in both English and Spanish.
