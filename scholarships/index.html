How Do State Education Funds Work for Families?

School choice policies allow families to apply state-allocated public education funding toward authorized customized distance-learning support services for their children.

💼

1. Education Savings Accounts (ESA / EFA / UFA)

State-funded accounts managed through a digital platform where families spend authorized amounts on qualifying educational services, curriculum, and diagnostic benchmarks.

📜

2. State Scholarships & School Choice Funds

Legislative school choice programs (such as AL CHOOSE, SC ESTF, or FL PEP) designed to empower parents to contract approved educational providers.

📱

3. Direct-Pay Digital Platforms

Approved platforms like ClassWallet or Odyssey where you find CHANAK TRAINUP EDUCATION INC and request payment authorization with your available program funds.

How Payment is Managed in Each Platform

Chanak is formally registered under its legal non-profit entity: CHANAK TRAINUP EDUCATION INC. Follow the workflow of your state platform to request payment authorizations smoothly.

💳 ClassWallet AZ · AL · SC · NH

ClassWallet (Pay Vendor)

Used by Arizona ESA, Alabama CHOOSE, South Carolina ESTF, and New Hampshire EFA.

  1. Chanak issues an itemized official PDF quote or invoice per student for the authorized service.
  2. Log in to your parent portal at app.classwallet.com and select 'Pay Vendor'.
  3. Search for our approved legal entity: CHANAK TRAINUP EDUCATION INC.
  4. Upload the invoice and submit for payment authorization using your available state funds.

Odyssey Marketplace

Official platform for Wyoming ESA and Utah Fits All Scholarship (UFA).

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions About State Education Funds

Clear answers to the most common questions from families using public school choice funds at Chanak International Academy.

? 1. How does payment work with state scholarship funds?

Eligible families may request payment for authorized services using available program funds. Approval depends on the service, student, and applicable program rules. In direct-pay states (such as Arizona, Alabama, South Carolina, or New Hampshire on ClassWallet, and Utah and Wyoming on Odyssey), Chanak provides the itemized quote or invoice for the family to submit directly through their platform.

? 2. How does my family locate Chanak in our portal?

Search for our exact approved legal entity: CHANAK TRAINUP EDUCATION INC. In ClassWallet, look under 'Pay Vendor'; in Odyssey, locate our profile in the official state provider directory.

? 3. What if my child's scholarship is approved mid-year?

Chanak maintains rolling admissions throughout the academic year. If your state funds are awarded midway through the year, we establish an individualized service plan and payment schedule aligned with your remaining available balance.

? 4. Do unused funds roll over or transfer to another sibling?

Policies vary by state. Generally, funds awarded to one student cannot be transferred to a sibling, but many state ESAs allow unused balances to roll over into subsequent academic years. Our team provides state-specific rules.

? 5. Does this support package include official cumulative transcripts and diploma?

This support package provides academic progress records, monthly progress reports, and pedagogical tracking of the contracted services. It does not constitute full school enrollment or independently award graduation credits or a High School diploma. Different full-enrollment academic pathways require their own contract, prerequisites, and program eligibility.

? 6. How are state scholarship services distinguished from private academic programs?

State scholarship funds are applied to contract specific authorized educational services (virtual guided support, academic mentoring, Life Skills cohorts, diagnostic assessment). Formal full-enrollment academic programs (such as private K-12 umbrella schooling or international dual diploma programs) operate under independent private contractual tracks.
