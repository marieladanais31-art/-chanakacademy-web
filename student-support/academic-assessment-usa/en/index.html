Academic assessment and individual planning: understand before moving ahead

Distinguishing placement, progress monitoring and required U.S. assessments.

A single grade cannot explain how a student learns. Academic assessment should lead to practical decisions: where to begin, what to reinforce and how to recognise progress.

Three different purposes

Placement identifies a suitable starting point. Progress monitoring checks what has been learned. Compliance assessment meets a requirement of an education pathway or funding programme. An internal Chanak assessment does not automatically replace a state, district or scholarship requirement.

Look at skills and context

Consider reading comprehension, writing, mathematical reasoning and English alongside previous work and family observations. For bilingual students, distinguish understanding of a subject from ability to express an answer in English.

Create an actionable plan

A Chanak individual academic plan should identify starting skills, goals, resources, supports, evidence and a review date. The family should understand what to do next and how progress will be assessed.

This academic plan is not automatically an IEP under U.S. special education law. Existing professional reports, accommodations or an IEP should be reviewed within the programme’s scope and with the relevant professionals.

Confirm the Florida pathway

Home Education and PEP have different registration and assessment obligations. Before purchasing a test, confirm the family’s programme, accepted assessment and administrator requirements. Learning placement and required assessment may be separate activities.

Academic support and clinical evaluation

Identifying learning needs is different from diagnosing a psychological condition. When specialist evaluation is needed, referral and professional credentials must be considered. This resource area does not announce a new clinical service or payment category.

Prepare for a consultation

Gather grade level, languages, records, work samples, existing reports and your question. Confirm service availability, scope, deliverables and price before contracting. Review the academic plan when evidence indicates a change is needed.
