Academic assessment and individual planning: understand before moving ahead

Distinguishing placement, progress monitoring and required U.S. assessments.

A single grade cannot explain how a student learns. Academic assessment should lead to practical decisions: where to begin, what to reinforce and how to recognise progress.

Three distinct areas: state law, scholarship rules, and school records

Testing requirements are not uniform for all homeschooling families, nor is Florida the only state with assessment guidelines. To identify which test applies, three separate areas must be distinguished:

  1. Homeschool legal requirements: Each state regulates home education under its own statutes and the legal option chosen by the family. Some states do not mandate testing, while others require periodic assessments that can be satisfied through various recognized options (certified teacher portfolio reviews, standardized tests, or professional evaluations).
  2. Scholarship and ESA/EFA evaluation requirements: Education Savings Accounts and school choice scholarship programs maintain their own accountability rules, which may differ from general state homeschool law. Any required norm-referenced test is submitted according to the scholarship funding organization guidelines.
  3. Chanak’s internal academic assessments and records: Chanak International Academy is a private school registered in Florida (FLDOE #134620). Its placement diagnostics, mastery checkpoints, and progress monitoring underpin the grades, official transcripts, and credits awarded by Chanak to students enrolled in its academic programs.
💡 Official policy on transcripts and credits:
“Students enrolled in our academic programs may receive an official transcript issued by Chanak and credits for completed and evaluated courses. Homeschool and scholarship assessment requirements vary by state. The acceptance or transfer of credits is subject to the policies of the receiving institution.”

Credits are awarded by Chanak as an American private school under its academic policies; they are not state-issued, nor is automatic transfer guaranteed by another school, district, university, or country. Stand-alone mentoring, tutoring, or Life Skills do not independently confer credits unless the student is enrolled and evaluated in a comprehensive academic program.

Look at skills and context

Consider reading comprehension, writing, mathematical reasoning and English alongside previous work and family observations. For bilingual students, distinguish understanding of a subject from ability to express an answer in English.

Create an actionable plan

A Chanak individual academic plan should identify starting skills, goals, resources, supports, evidence and a review date. The family should understand what to do next and how progress will be assessed.

This academic plan is not automatically an IEP under U.S. special education law. Existing professional reports, accommodations or an IEP should be reviewed within the programme’s scope and with the relevant professionals.

Florida pathways: Home Education vs. PEP and Scholarships

In Florida, distinct legal and funding pathways exist; none of them represent a uniform obligation to submit exams directly to the state:

  • Florida Home Education (s. 1002.41, F.S.): Requires an annual educational progress evaluation that can be met through multiple valid methods: a portfolio review by a Florida-certified teacher, a nationally normed standardized assessment administered by a certified tester, a psychological evaluation, or other mutually agreed district assessment.
  • Florida PEP (Personalized Education Program under FES-UA / FTC): Requires an annual norm-referenced test from the state-approved assessment list. Results are submitted directly to the Scholarship Funding Organization (SFO, e.g. Step Up for Students) managing the account, not directly to the state.
  • Scholarship participation: Specific scholarship programs may include particular progress reporting obligations to maintain funding eligibility.

Before ordering an assessment, verify your specific legal status, eligible instruments, and qualified administrator guidelines with Chanak advisors and the scholarship administrator.

Academic support and clinical evaluation

Identifying learning needs is different from diagnosing a psychological condition. When specialist evaluation is needed, referral and professional credentials must be considered. This resource area does not announce a new clinical service or payment category.

Prepare for a consultation

Gather grade level, languages, records, work samples, existing reports and your question. Confirm service availability, scope, deliverables and price before contracting. Review the academic plan when evidence indicates a change is needed.
