About Us

At Chanak TrainUp Education Inc. (d/b/a Chanak International Academy), we walk alongside home-educating families with premier personalized mentoring and holistic character formation.

We anchor character education in a biblical worldview that nurtures integrity, lifelong purpose, and a heart for service in every student.

We implement the 60/20/20 learning model: 60% Core Curriculum, 20% Heritage Language & Cultural Studies, and 20% Life Skills & Biblical Worldview. In ESA support, the student plan documents how the family approaches each area and the included scope, without promising uncontracted classes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to essential questions from families utilizing state education funds.

Can I pay for Chanak programs using my Arizona ESA funds? +

Yes. Chanak TrainUp Education Inc. is an approved vendor for the Arizona ESA program. We issue an itemized invoice per student paid directly via Pay Vendor on ClassWallet. Each program administrator determines which expenses are eligible.

What is the difference between Full-Time Online School and Academic Support? +

Full-Time Online School provides formal school enrollment under Florida Private School Code FLDOE #134620, cumulative student academic records, high school transcripts with validated credits, and an American High School diploma upon graduation. Academic Support packages provide weekly mentoring, formative feedback, and Life Skills workshops for families directing their own home education program without granting transcripts or diplomas.

Can I select monthly or quarterly billing? +

Yes. Academic Support packages can be billed monthly ($275, $320, $365) or quarterly ($825, $960, $1,095 for three months). Full-Time Online School tuition is billed quarterly ($1,125, $1,237.50, $1,350) or through requested monthly payment options.

What are the live class schedules? +

Live Life Skills workshops, intensive language classes, and mentoring meetings are scheduled during confirmed class times coordinated with the family. Teachers are not available 24/7, though the virtual learning platform is accessible 24/7.

How are curriculum books and study materials purchased? +

Families purchase general curriculum materials directly from publishers after receiving our recommended list during the initial educational assessment. Chanak Life Skills program materials are provided at no extra cost.

What is the cancellation and refund policy? +

You may cancel with advance notice prior to the upcoming billing period. Invoicing is per student via Pay Vendor. If a refund of state funds is due for unrendered services, funds are returned directly to the student's state account in ClassWallet; refunds are never disbursed in cash or to private accounts.

In what language are sessions and academic support delivered? +

Support is fully bilingual in English and Spanish. Mentoring, assessments, and family communications are offered in Spanish or English according to family preference. Group Life Skills sessions encourage fluent bilingual communication.
