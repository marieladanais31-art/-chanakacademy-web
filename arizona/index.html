About Us

At Chanak TrainUp Education Inc. (d/b/a Chanak International Academy), we provide premier American distance education backed by personalized mentoring and holistic character formation.

We anchor character education in a biblical worldview that nurtures integrity, lifelong purpose, and a heart for service in every student.

We implement the 60/20/20 learning model: 60% Core Curriculum, 20% Heritage Language & Cultural Studies, and 20% Life Skills & Biblical Worldview. Attention to families is 100% bilingual in Spanish and English.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for Arizona families utilizing Empowerment Scholarship Account funds.

Can I pay for Chanak programs using my Arizona ESA funds? +

Yes. Chanak TrainUp Education Inc. (d/b/a Chanak International Academy) is an approved vendor for the Arizona ESA program. Invoices under category "Tuition and Fees" are submitted and paid directly through Pay Vendor on ClassWallet. Each program administrator determines which expenses are eligible.

What is included in Full-Time Online K–12 Tuition & Academic Support? +

Full-Time Online School provides formal school enrollment under FLDOE #134620, candidate for MSA-CESS accreditation, assigned academic mentor, live Life Skills, Character & Leadership classes, Academic Language access, attendance tracking, cumulative records, and an American High School diploma upon fulfilling graduation requirements.

How is tuition billed? +

Tuition is billed in four quarterly installments: $1,125 per quarter for Elementary (Grades 1–5), $1,237.50 per quarter for Middle School (Grades 6–8), and $1,350 per quarter for High School (Grades 9–12), corresponding to annual tuition of $4,500, $4,950, and $5,400 respectively. A one-time initial assessment and setup fee of $295 is billed separately.

When do educational services begin? +

Services begin after payment has been received and reconciled in Chanak’s bank account and the schedule has been confirmed with the family.

How are curriculum books and study materials purchased? +

Families purchase general curriculum textbooks directly from publishers according to the student’s learning plan. Chanak Life Skills program materials and Academic Language access are provided at no additional cost.

What is the cancellation and refund policy? +

Enrollment may be cancelled with advance notice prior to the upcoming billing period. Invoices are issued per student via Pay Vendor. If a refund of state funds is required for unrendered services, funds are returned directly to the family's state account in ClassWallet according to program rules.

In what language are sessions and academic support delivered? +

Support and family communications are 100% bilingual in English and Spanish according to family preference.
