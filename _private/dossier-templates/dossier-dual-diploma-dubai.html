02 · THE PROGRAM

What exactly is the U.S. Dual Diploma?

A complementary academic pathway leading toward a U.S. High School Diploma issued by Chanak International Academy, alongside the student's current education, when applicable.

How it works

The student continues their current school without interruption. Chanak reviews the student's academic profile and defines a personalized route. The student completes Chanak's requirements — approximately 5 hours per week — with academic mentoring and progress tracked through the Student Information System (SIS). Upon successful completion of the program requirements, Chanak issues the diploma.

What this is NOT

Not a basic English course
Does not replace the student's current diploma
Not the same as Chanak's Off-Campus program
No automatic credit recognition without evaluation
Not a guarantee of university admission

05 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What families usually ask

Does it guarantee university admission?

No. It provides academic documentation that may be considered as part of admission processes. Requirements vary by university, country and program; the final decision is always made by the receiving institution.

Is Chanak accredited?

Chanak is registered with the Florida Department of Education (FLDOE #134620) and is currently a Candidate for Accreditation with MSA-CESS. Candidate status does not constitute accreditation.

How many hours per week?

Approximately 5 hours per week on average, confirmed for each student during the admissions process.

Can my child keep their current school?

Yes. The Dual Diploma is designed to run alongside the student's current education, when applicable.
