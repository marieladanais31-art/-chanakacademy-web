One calling

Three ways to teach with purpose

Every home has its own rhythm and calling. At Chanak we honor the authority of parents to decide how their children are formed: the foundation of faith and the accredited academic standard are the same; what changes is the way the journey is walked. That is why we come alongside you in three approaches —at home, with a dual diploma, or in community— so you can build their future around your family’s reality.

Frequently asked questions

What families ask first

Is Chanak International Academy accredited?

Yes. Chanak International Academy is accredited by the Middle States Association Commissions on Elementary and Secondary Schools (MSA-CESS) and is a private school registered with the Florida Department of Education (FLDOE #134620). It is operated by Chanak TrainUp Education, Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

What is the difference between Guided Homeschool, Full Enrollment, Dual Diploma and Microschool?

In Guided Homeschool the family leads daily learning with an individual plan and monthly family mentoring. In Full Enrollment the student is enrolled full time, with live classes and weekly mentoring. In Dual Diploma the student stays at their local school and adds the U.S. High School Diploma. Microschool is a partnership with communities of about 10 or more families learning in small in-person groups.

How is tuition structured?

Tuition varies depending on the chosen program (Guided Homeschool, Full Enrollment, or Dual Diploma), the student’s grade, and country of residence. For complete details, monthly or annual payment options, and enrollment inclusions, please visit our tuition page or each program’s dedicated page.

Is the diploma valid in my country?

The High School Diploma and transcript are issued by Chanak as an accredited U.S. school. It is an academic credential; recognition, equivalency or apostille procedures are only needed when a specific authority requires them for a specific use. Compliance with local school regulations is the responsibility of each family in its country.

How is Chanak related to Dallas Baptist University?

Chanak is a member of Dallas Baptist University’s Global Community, a network of 280+ schools in 50 countries. Membership provides access to teacher training, English programs and scholarship opportunities according to each DBU program. DBU does not accredit Chanak, and membership does not guarantee admission or scholarships.

Can I use my state ESA funds in the United States?

Chanak is an approved provider in Alabama, Arizona, Florida, New Hampshire, South Carolina, Utah and Wyoming. Student eligibility, available funds and authorization of each purchase are determined by each state’s program.

What are Mastery Learning and the 60/20/20 model?

Mastery Learning means students move forward when they master each objective. The 60/20/20 model divides the plan into 60% U.S. core curriculum, 20% local extension (the language, history and culture of the family’s country) and 20% Life Skills, character and biblical worldview.
