Terms

Annual Enrollment Fee (includes academic diagnostic assessment, Individualized Education Plan and onboarding/orientation)

The annual enrollment fee includes the initial academic assessment, the Individualized Education Plan (IEP/PEI), and student/family onboarding. Once paid, the fee is non-refundable, except where applicable law grants mandatory withdrawal, cancellation, refund, or other non-waivable rights.
Is the annual enrollment fee paid only once?

Yes. It is one fee per student per academic year, regardless of whether you pay monthly, quarterly or annually. It renews at the start of each new academic year and we tell you beforehand.

What does the annual enrollment fee include?

The initial academic diagnostic, the Individualized Education Plan (IEP/PEI) and student and family onboarding.

Are materials included?

It depends on the plan and curriculum chosen (A.C.E., LIFEPAC, Christian Light or Chanak Flex). We tell you in writing which materials you need and their cost before you enroll.

Can I pay with ESA funds in the United States?

In states where Chanak is an approved provider you can request payment with your state funds. Eligibility and each authorization are decided by your state’s program.
