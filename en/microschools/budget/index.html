Budget calculator for your Microschool

An original Chanak tool for founders: start-up investment, monthly costs, income and break-even. Fields start empty and nothing you type leaves your browser.

The result is the partner’s own estimate, not a commitment by Chanak. It is not an offer or legal or tax advice. Chanak’s partnership terms are delivered in writing in the partnership proposal.

Tips for budgeting wisely

  • Start small: share or borrow space from a church or community.
  • Stagger staff hiring as the group grows.
  • Set tuition with your budget in hand, not out of fear of empty seats.
  • Review the budget every month: a budget that is not updated stops being useful.
  • Aim for a small surplus from the third month to build a reserve.
