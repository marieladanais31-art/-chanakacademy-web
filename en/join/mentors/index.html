Mentors and collaborators

If you are moved to walk with a student until they truly understand, there is a place for you here.

What a mentor does

  • Academic follow-up: reviews each student’s progress, weekly goals and difficulties.
  • Life Skills: supports the growth of character, habits and life project.
  • Mastery Learning: helps students move forward when they master each objective, not by calendar.
  • 60/20/20 model: integrates the American academic core, the local extension and the biblical worldview.

Requirements to become a mentor

Working with minors calls for care. These are the requirements:

  • Up-to-date résumé and references.
  • Evaluation interview.
  • Background check and child protection screening, according to the country.
  • Mandatory institutional training.
  • Knowledge of the Mastery Learning model, the IEP/PEI, the SIS, Chanak Flex and academic follow-up.
  • Evaluation and approval before working with families.
  • Commitment to Chanak’s Christian identity and institutional policies.

Training with Chanak TrainUp

Before walking with students you complete Chanak’s training to apply Mastery Learning, the IEP/PEI, the SIS, Chanak Flex and academic follow-up. Mentor certification does not guarantee hiring. Chanak TrainUp ↗

A separate agreement

Being a mentor or collaborator is a separate agreement from being an ambassador. Compensation is set by contract, according to the services actually provided (family mentoring, individual sessions or academic coordination). No rates are published. It is not the same as being a Chanak employee unless there is an express contract.
