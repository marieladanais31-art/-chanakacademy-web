Chanak Ambassador Program

Help families find a Christian education that forms the whole person.

Chanak International Academy supports students through Off-Campus (Guided Homeschool), Dual Diploma and Life Skills. As an ambassador, you share what you already know with the families who need it. We give you materials, training and clear terms in writing.

Who can be an ambassador?

You do not need a large audience. We look for people and communities who already walk with families and share our mission.

Churches and communities

Pastors and leaders who want to offer their families an educational option faithful to their faith.

Co-ops and homeschool groups

Leaders who know first-hand what families are looking for and need.

Educators and creators

Mentors, teachers and content creators who explain well what they use.

Chanak families

Parents who already live the program and want to share their real experience. Not open to families who receive funds from a state education program.

How it works

1

Apply

Tell us who you are, who you serve and how you plan to share Chanak.

2

Get approved and receive your code

We review every application. If approved, you receive your ambassador code, your own link, approved texts and resources, and a kickoff webinar.

3

Share with integrity

You guide families to their first step. Admissions and enrollment are always handled by the Chanak team.

What you receive

  • An ambassador code and your own link, so every enrollment is registered in your name.
  • A kit of approved texts, images and guides, in English and Spanish.
  • A kickoff webinar on Off-Campus, Dual Diploma and Life Skills.
  • A team contact to answer your questions.
  • Between $50 and $150 for each enrolled student, depending on the program, paid in US dollars by PayPal (one payment per student).

Clear terms

The full terms are given in writing to each approved ambassador.

  • What counts: a confirmed and paid enrollment, not just a sign-up.
  • How much: between $50 and $150 per student, depending on the program. Paid once per student.
  • Attribution: the period from when the family uses your code or link is confirmed in writing before you start.
  • Payments: in US dollars (USD), by PayPal, once a month; the minimum payout is confirmed in writing before you start.
  • State programs: compensation for students funded by a state program depends on each program’s rules and is confirmed in writing before you start.
  • Who cannot participate: people or families who receive funds from a state education program, in any state, cannot participate in the program.

How we speak about Chanak

Families trust what we tell them. That is why every ambassador follows the same guidelines.

Do

  • Use only approved texts and materials.
  • Say clearly that you receive compensation when it applies.
  • Explain accurately what Off-Campus, Dual Diploma and Life Skills are.
  • Use only the official wording: “Accredited by MSA-CESS”.
  • Send families to the official website and the admissions team.

Do not

  • Promise aid, scholarships, funding or state program approvals.
  • State that Dallas Baptist University accredits Chanak, or promise admission, dual enrollment or scholarships.
  • Sign, negotiate or speak on behalf of the organization.
  • Collect, store or publish student or family data.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a job or volunteering?

Neither. It is an independent collaboration and does not create an employment relationship. If you want to work with students as a mentor or volunteer, there is a separate option with its own training and agreement.

Do I need a large audience?

No. We review each application based on your mission, who you serve and how you plan to share Chanak.

How much is recognized for each enrollment, and how is it paid?

Between $50 and $150 per enrolled student, depending on the program, paid once. It is paid in US dollars by PayPal. The full terms are given in writing before you begin.

Can I participate if my family receives funds from a state education program?

No. People and families who receive funds from a state education program, in any state, cannot participate in the ambassador program.

What happens with families’ data?

Families register directly with Chanak. Ambassadors do not collect or store their data.

Can I share from any country?

Tell us your country when you apply. Taxes and rules can differ depending on where you live, and we review them with you before the agreement.
