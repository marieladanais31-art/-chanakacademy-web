Ambassador Program Terms (draft)

Draft pending legal review. This is not a final or binding document.

Nature of the collaboration

The ambassador collaborates independently. The program does not create an employment, partnership or agency relationship with Chanak TrainUp Education, Inc. The ambassador may not sign, negotiate or speak on behalf of the organization.

Who may participate

People and organizations approved by Chanak after reviewing their application. People or families who receive funds from a state education program, in any state, may not participate.

Compensation

Between $50 and $150 per enrolled student, depending on the program, once per student, and only on enrollments that have actually been collected and not refunded. If an enrollment is refunded, the associated compensation is not earned or is offset in the next settlement. A commission percentage, if one ever applies, is still to be defined.

Attribution

Attribution window of 30 days from the last valid click on the ambassador’s link (or use of their code). Attribution respects applicable privacy and consent requirements: only the referral identifier is recorded, with no tracking of families on other sites, and optional measurement requires their consent.

Settlement and payment

Monthly settlement. Payment within 30 days after the close of the corresponding period, in US dollars by PayPal. Proposed minimum payout: US$50, pending approval.

Representation

Ambassadors promote and recommend Chanak’s programs, but do not legally represent the institution and cannot guarantee admissions, scholarships, accreditations or academic recognition.

How Chanak is described

Only with approved texts and materials. Official wording: “Accredited by MSA-CESS” and “Private school registered in Florida (FLDOE #134620)”. Do not state that Dallas Baptist University accredits Chanak, and do not promise admissions, dual enrollment, scholarships, aid or state program approvals.

Personal data

Families register directly with Chanak. The ambassador does not collect, store or publish student or family data.

Taxes and local rules

Each ambassador is responsible for their own tax obligations. They are reviewed with each person before the agreement according to their country of residence.

Termination and changes

Chanak may withdraw approval if these rules are not followed. Final terms are given in writing to each approved ambassador.
