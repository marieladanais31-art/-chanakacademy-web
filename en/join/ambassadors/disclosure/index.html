Compensation disclosure (draft)

Draft pending legal review. This is not a final or binding document.

What it is

Chanak ambassadors may receive financial compensation when a family they have guided completes an enrollment. This page makes that public.

When the ambassador must say so

Whenever they recommend Chanak in a conversation, post, video or message where they may receive compensation, clearly and visibly. For example: “I am a Chanak ambassador and may receive compensation if you enroll with my code.”

What does not change

Compensation does not change the price the family pays or the admission conditions. Admissions and enrollment are always handled by the Chanak team.
