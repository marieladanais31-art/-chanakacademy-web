EDUCATIONAL MODEL

What is a Chanak Microschool?

A Chanak Microschool is a small in-person learning community (typically 5 to 12 students) combining the warmth, adaptability, and healthy peer socialization of a local cohort with the accredited academic structure and official standing of an American private school.

👥

Small Cohorts

Individualized attention where every learner is known by name, learning style, and personal strengths, moving beyond the crowding of conventional classrooms.

🎓

Accredited Standing

Backing from Chanak International Academy (FLDOE #134620), fully accredited by MSA-CESS, issuing official transcripts and an American High School Diploma track.

🌟

Routine & Socialization

Structured daily cadence with collaborative inquiry, group discussions, character development, and meaningful peer friendships in a safe setting.

HOLISTIC PEDAGOGY

How Your Child Learns

Our instructional design centers on each learner through four structural pillars crafted for conceptual understanding and character development.

🎯

Mastery Learning

Students advance only when they have demonstrated comprehensive mastery of foundational concepts. No hidden gaps accumulate; learners proceed at their natural pace.

📐

The 60/20/20 Model

A balanced curricular framework: 60% Core U.S. Curriculum under accredited standards, 20% Local Extension (heritage language, history, geography), and 20% Life Skills, character, and worldview.

🧭

Chanak-Certified Coordinator

Each cohort is facilitated on-site by a trained coordinator certified by Chanak, equipped in student mentoring, adaptive pacing, learning atmosphere, and individual follow-up.

💻

Integrated SIS Platform

Chanak's Student Information System (SIS) centralizes the student's individualized educational plan (IEP), weekly goals, assessment outcomes, and official school records.

TUITION & FUNDING

How Tuition is Structured

Funding pathways depend on your country of residence and the participation framework chosen by your family.

🇺🇸 United States: State Scholarship Funds (ESA / EFA)

In states where Chanak participates as an authorized education service provider (e.g., Florida Step Up PEP/FES-UA, Utah Fits All, Arizona ClassWallet, New Hampshire EFA), families may apply their state education scholarship accounts toward eligible services.

Important Program Notice: In-person services currently in the approval process for each program. Funding eligibility and authorized use depend on specific state program guidelines and scholarship administrator policies.

🌐 International: Local Center Fee

In Spain, Mexico, Panama, and other regions, families remit tuition directly to the local partner learning center managing physical facilities and on-site staff. Chanak provides the accredited U.S. curriculum, digital platform, and official school transcripts.

To view direct enrollment tuition and distance-learning fees for fully virtual tracks, please visit our homepage tuition section.
